Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our n8n automation tool ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you connect and interact with our application integrated with TikTok API Services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize our application via TikTok Login Kit, we may collect the following limited information:

  • Basic Account Information: Your TikTok open_id, display name, and profile picture (via user.info.basic).
  • Publishing Data: Media files (videos, images) and captions that you explicitly schedule or trigger to publish via our n8n workflows.
  • Access Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens required to maintain API connection.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and allow secure connection to your TikTok account.
  • To execute user-initiated automation workflows in n8n for posting video content directly to TikTok (using TikTok Content Posting API).
  • To display status logs and posting history within your personal n8n workspace.

We do NOT sell, rent, share, or trade your personal data or TikTok account details with any third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens and user credentials are encrypted and stored locally/privately within your n8n instance. We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification of your data.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

We retain your API tokens only for as long as your n8n workflow is active. You can revoke our application's access to your TikTok account at any time by:

  • Navigating to your TikTok mobile app -> Settings -> Security -> App Permissions, and removing authorization for this application.
  • Deleting the API credentials from your n8n credentials manager.

5. Compliance with TikTok API Terms

Our application adheres strictly to the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official repository or administrator channel.
